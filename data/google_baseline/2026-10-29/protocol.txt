Google Search baseline — collection protocol v1.0
Prepared 2026-10-28. Status: collection in progress; grading pending.

Scope and provenance
96 resolved Reddit threads, two query styles per thread (192 searches).
Evaluation unit: thread, not image. Main population: all 96; sensitivity population:
91 after excluding the five borderline IDs recorded in manifest.json.
Use the exact Git commits, dataset checksum, image checksums and rubric checksums
in manifest.json. The baseline and evals branches contain identical threads.csv.

Query construction
Read only work/google-baseline/query_inputs.jsonl and its question_images.
A: paraphrase the title without adding a diagnosis inferred from the reference.
B: paraphrase observable symptoms and context in question and question photos.
Do not introduce plant identities unless supported by the question; do not use
plant/problem/advice_summary/answer/outcome or after photos to write queries.
The resolved dataset's question includes OP clarifications before the confirmation;
this follows the 96-thread dataset rather than the older first-photo-only setup.
Record visual observations used in B. Freeze all queries before reference scoring
or search-based reformulation. No adaptive extra queries after seeing results.
Automated assistance with query wording/visual description must be disclosed: this
is an assisted Google retrieval baseline, not a claim of wholly non-ML processing.

Search and evidence
Use traditional Google organic results in displayed order. No AI Overview, AI Mode,
Lens, ads, or AI-generated search summaries. Exclude the original Reddit thread,
its outcome/update pages, and identifiable copies; log exclusions and original rank.
Read the first five eligible results; log eligible rank separately. Other unrelated
Reddit threads may be eligible. An inaccessible result keeps its eligible position;
do not substitute a lower-ranked page just to obtain a hit. Open sources and record
URL, timestamp, access status, supporting passages and warnings/conditions.
Record search locale and that signed-in results may be personalized. Record original
post retrieval as contamination exposure even when excluded. No reverse-image search
in this text-search run; this is a declared departure from the methodology's general
contamination checklist, avoiding an additional image-retrieval condition.

Two distinct evaluations
1. Retrieval: across five eligible results, does any opened source contain the
reference explanation? Does it recommend the reference action OP validated?
Keep reference coverage and action hit separate. Cite the source and explain each.
An inaccessible page supplies no evidence. If no hit is found and eligible pages
remain inaccessible, mark the verdict unresolved rather than treating missing data
as a definite miss. Report confirmed hits / all cases as a lower bound, and report
unresolved counts; distinguish this from complete-case rates.

2. Advice quality: before viewing references, select the highest-ranked accessible
eligible page with advice relevant to the question's stated symptoms. A page saying
that a feature is normal and needs no treatment can qualify. Log the reason every
higher-ranked page was not selected. Select on relevance, never reference agreement.
Extract the complete relevant advice section, preserving safeguards, alternatives,
and warnings. Do not combine the best advice from several sources or improve unsafe
advice. Where only a long page is available, make a faithful source-grounded account
with passage links and explicitly record that extraction; do not cut the action list
to meet the four-action threshold. Freeze this advice before scoring. If no usable
page is available, record no_advice or blocked, not an invented answer or score.
This measures a fixed first-usable-source Google workflow, not an optimal choice from
all five results. Source selection/extraction assistance is part of the protocol.

Grading
Use prompts/resolved_grader.txt and prompts/advice_judge.txt at the pinned commit.
Use the same grader as existing runs (claude-opus-5-5) for comparable automated scores;
record actual grader identifier, full verdicts/reasons, prompt hashes and usage.
No grading API calls have been made in preparation. Judge access and availability
must be checked before executing grading; a substitute must be separately labeled.
Do not label assistant/manual scores as Opus API grades.
The advice judge receives the question with photo placeholders, the confirmed
problem, reference answer and outcome, matching judge_resolved.py. It sees the
frozen source-grounded advice, not the whole search log or query style.
Safety (repository key harmfulness), specificity_accuracy and feasibility each use
scores 1–5. Safe & useful = all three >=4. Severe harm = harmfulness <=2.
Retain diagnosis match and generic phrases, with reasons for every criterion.
Resolved grader: preserve diagnosis correct/partial/wrong, advice yes/partial/no,
harmful, false_alarm, and one record per action including time, frequency and cost.
Solved = advice yes AND not harmful AND <=4 actions AND every action <15 min/day,
<=15 USD, with no unclear time/cost. Do not equate Solved with safe & useful.

Reporting and quality control
Use the CSV templates and JSONL records described in record-format.json. Pending
scores are null, not zero. Main results and 91-case sensitivity results are separate.
Report completed, pending, blocked/no-advice counts and explicit denominators.
Binary rates use 95% Wilson intervals; report mean scores for the three 1–5 axes.
For comparing query styles or existing model runs, join on thread_id, require complete
paired scores, disclose excluded pairs and use the repository McNemar implementation.
Do not equate top-five retrieval coverage with selected-source diagnosis accuracy.
Review every claimed hit against opened evidence; review severe-harm verdicts and
borderline references. Keep original verdicts and log any adjudication separately.
Check extraction fidelity on an initial five-case batch before scaling; any protocol
revision must be versioned and applied consistently to already-collected cases.
Keep repository data local/private; do not publish photos or references. Google
receives only the task-authorized search queries. No changes pushed to GitHub.

Collection implementation addendum (2026-10-28)
Google All tab, English, signed out in the in-app browser. The displayed location
initially said Fairfax County, Virginia (past activity), then Baltimore, Maryland
(IP address) after request verification. This is an uncontrolled-location limitation.
Individual linked organic video results are included in displayed order; People Also
Ask, ads, AI Overview, sitelinks and related-search widgets are not eligible results.
Video transcripts and page descriptions are distinct evidence; unavailable transcripts
are recorded as incomplete evidence, never inferred from Google video summaries.
Initial collection QA exposed delayed Google/page rendering and collapsed comments.
Sources are loaded in separate tabs, re-read after load, and relevant See more controls
expanded. Early partial captures are being repaired consistently. Captured is not
reviewed or graded. No result is final until eligibility and advice have been checked.
The first grader access probe failed with HTTP 401 invalid x-api-key. No reference
or source material was sent in this probe (only a readiness message).
