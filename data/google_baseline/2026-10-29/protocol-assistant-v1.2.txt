Final audit amendment (post-hoc; 2026-10-29)

All 192 initial evaluations were completed before this consistency audit.
Original grades are preserved in work/google-baseline/assistant-grades-before-audit.
Every changed decision is recorded in adjudication-log.json. No frozen query or
source-advice selection was rewritten after references were read.

A hit requires an executable central action, with appropriate direction/mechanism.
Functional equivalents or one substantive component of an OP-confirmed bundle may
count; a hit does not require the entire bundle or imply safe advice. Generic desired
conditions, diagnosis alone, incidental aftercare and a different mechanism do not
suffice. In particular, root soaking does not equal foliar spraying, and generic
indirect-light preference does not establish an increase in light. Prior failed
interventions cannot substitute for the subsequently successful escalation.

Primary retrieval results exclude two same-owner cross-posts identified during
review (query 17 rank 1; query 142 rank 2). No lower result was substituted after
reference exposure. Query 17 has no independently verified hit and an unreadable
remaining result, so is unresolved. Query 142 has other independent hits. Raw
pre-exclusion retrieval labels and the contaminated selected answer remain saved.
Selected-source metrics include all 96 per style for descriptive continuity, with
an explicit separate sensitivity excluding query 17's contaminated selection.

Action minutes measure active owner handling, excluding passive soaking, draining,
and callusing intervals. Original elapsed estimates are preserved on changed actions.
The report also supplies Solved using those original elapsed estimates. Estimates
are not measured task times or current price quotations. Existing equipment is free
when the question explicitly establishes ownership. Capacity remains <=4 tasks,
each <15 minutes/day and <=$15, with no unclear estimate, per repository definition.

Limitations retained: one assistant authored queries, selected advice and graded;
no independent rater or specialist adjudication. Extraction scope varied between
complete broad guides and focused relevant sections. The frozen selections were

references establish recovery without explicit action attribution. They remain in
the designated 96-case population with flags, not invented confirmation. Separate
explanatory reference coverage was not scored and is null, not zero.
