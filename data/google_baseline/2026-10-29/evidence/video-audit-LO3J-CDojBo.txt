For trees I'm just growing on, I usually use a commercial cactus potting soil, mixed with about one third perlite, while for my more developed bonsai I use my own bonsai soil recipe. I wanted to find out if it made any difference, so I planted two cloned trident maple cuttings in two pots with each of the mixes. I also give my recipe for bonsai soil, which contains akadama, pumice, lava rock and composted pine bark. If you have a different recipe that works for you, congratulations!
