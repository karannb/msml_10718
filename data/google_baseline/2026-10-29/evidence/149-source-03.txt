    - paragraph: Yes, you should give it your best attempt. Plants are living and like people, they have an inherent desire to survive.     - paragraph:
      - generic: "Diagnosis:"
      - text: "Overwatering. "
    - paragraph:
      - generic: "Cure:"
      - text: Stop watering so much.
    - paragraph: Your plant is drowning in water love. This is the number one reason indoor plants die. Wet soil will confirm that you’ve been overwatering.
    - paragraph: If a plant is overwatered, the roots will rot and “watering it regularly” only makes things worse. Many times rotted roots allow a pathogen into the plant and soon enough, it’s a goner.
    - paragraph:
      - generic: "How to save it:"
      - text: Remove any obviously rotted roots and replace soil that has turned to mud. Allow the soil to dry until it is slightly damp to bone dry.     - paragraph: If the plant is withering due to lack of water, hydrate the soil by putting the entire pot into a sink or bucket full of water for 15 to 30 minutes. Let it drain thoroughly and don’t let your plant sit in water for more than a day. Then set a calendar reminder to water – or get a plant that needs the bare minimum of watering, like a succulent.
