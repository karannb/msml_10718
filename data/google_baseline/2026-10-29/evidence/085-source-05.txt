    - text: That tall flower spike is basil switching from leaf production to reproduction
    - img "🌿"
    - text: "What I usually do depends on what I want:"
    - img "✂️"
    - text: If I want tender basil leaves longer, I pinch the flower spikes off as soon as I notice them.
    - img "🌱"
    - text: Cutting just above a set of leaves encourages the plant to branch again.
    - img "💧"
    - text: Regular harvesting and consistent moisture can help keep usable growth coming.
    - img "🌼"
    - text: If I want basil seed instead, I let a few flower stalks mature and dry on the plant.
    - generic: You don’t have to remove every flower. Keep some for seed and pinch the rest if fresh leaves are the priority.
