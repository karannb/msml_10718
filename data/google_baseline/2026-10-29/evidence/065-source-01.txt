    - listitem: Root-bound plant - If the peace lily becomes pot-bound, it won’t absorb moisture and nutrients properly from the soil, which will cause yellowing leaves.

    - link "repotting your peace lily":
      - /url: https://www.livelyroot.com/blogs/plant-care/how-to-repot-a-peace-lily

  - paragraph: "Even though peace lily plants are fairly easy-going and low-maintenance, they may develop visible problems.   - list:
    - listitem: "Soil: Peace lilies grow best in aerated, well-draining soil with some moisture retention capacity."
