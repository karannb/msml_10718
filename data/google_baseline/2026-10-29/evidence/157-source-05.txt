  - paragraph: Information overload!
  - paragraph: Sometimes it does not matter if a seed grown fruit tree bears fruit, sometimes it's just nice to experience with new seeds or enjoy the foliage of plants nurseries usual do not carry.
  - paragraph:
    - text: "The steps:"
    - text: "-Remove/brush all pulp from seeds - pulp=mold"
    - text: "-Wash the seeds with warm water, add a few drops of dish liquid"
    - text: "-Rinse seeds well"
    - text: "-Wrap seed in a clean paper towel or coffee filter"
    - text: "-Moisten with distilled water, rain water will do also,"
    - text: (adding a few drops of H2O2/Hyrdogen peroxide assists with fungus control, but it's not a requirement)
    - text: "-Keep at 70-75F in a zip lock bag."
    - text: "-Change the dressing every other day, moisten"
    - text: "-Keep damp but not wet - too much water=rot"
    - text: "-Germination in 2-6 weeks"
  - paragraph: Good luck!

  - paragraph: Lychee doesn’t grow true to seed kinda like apple slices. You need to start the tree with a branch from the mother. If you want the same fruit and taste. If you’re growing it from seed skip paper towels start it right in a pot. Should sprout in a week or three. If it takes more than a month it’s bad. Try to plant the seed within a week of taking it from the fruit. And in about 10-25 years you’ll have some fruit.
