        - generic: Watering that often is too much and the size of the pot is too big, which then holds cold, damp soil around the roots. If it is about to break I think it has rotted. I would restart by cutting above the rotten stem, remove the lower leaves, let the new cut dry for a couple of hours to seal the wound, then sit it in water or moist soil to propagate. Warmth is your friend when trying to encourage roots. Change the water weekly if you use the water method.
