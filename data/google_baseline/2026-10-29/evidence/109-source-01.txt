        - generic: Leaves are little solar factories for plants with the green chlorophyll converting light to sugars. Cutting leaves off is almost never helpful. Variegated leaves have less green to feed the plant, so those need even more light. Your plant's leaves are full green because with the low light, it needs to maximize chlorophyll. The big spaces and smaller new leaves also show low light. If you use lights, they must be strong, and within one foot directly over your plant. USB types are too weak. Leave them on 12-16 hrs/day. If using natural light, here's a good guide to placement.
