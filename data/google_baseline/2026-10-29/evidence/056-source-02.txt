- heading "Also Known As" [level=2]
- paragraph: Oxlip, Cowslip, Key flower, Primrose
- heading "About This Plant" [level=2]
- paragraph: Primula elatior elatior is a species of flowering plant in the family Primulaceae, native to central and southern Europe. It is a herbaceous perennial growing to 30 cm (12 in) tall by 30 cm (12 in) wide, with rosettes of wrinkled, hairy leaves and clusters of pale yellow flowers in early spring.
- heading "Plant Care Instructions" [level=2]
- heading "Outdoor Size Range" [level=3]
- paragraph: 12-18 inches (30-45 cm)
- heading "Light Requirements" [level=3]
- paragraph: Oxlips prefer partial shade to full shade.
- heading "Humidity Needs" [level=3]
- paragraph: Oxlips prefer a moderate to high level of humidity. Consider using a humidifier or placing a tray of water near the plant to increase humidity levels.
- heading "Fertilizing Schedule" [level=3]
- paragraph: Fertilize with a balanced, water-soluble fertilizer once a month during the growing season.
- heading "Pruning Guidelines" [level=3]
- paragraph: Prune spent flowers and yellowing leaves to encourage new growth.
- heading "Common Pests and Diseases" [level=3]
- paragraph: Oxlips are susceptible to slugs and snails. They may also be affected by powdery mildew.
- heading "Indoor Size Range" [level=3]
- paragraph: 6-8 inches (15-20 cm)
- heading "Watering Schedule" [level=3]
- paragraph: Keep the soil consistently moist but not waterlogged. Watering once a week should suffice.
- heading "Temperature Range" [level=3]
- paragraph: Oxlips prefer cooler temperatures ranging from 50-70°F (10-21°C).
- heading "Growing Season" [level=3]
- paragraph: Spring
- heading "Growing Difficulty Level" [level=3]
- paragraph: Moderate
- heading "USDA Hardiness Zones" [level=3]
- paragraph: 4-8
- heading "Did You Know?" [level=2]
- paragraph: The Oxlip was once considered a hybrid between the Primrose and the Cowslip, but is now recognized as a distinct species.
