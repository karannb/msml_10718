        - generic: Pruning and more light stimulate growth. Look on YouTube how to prune money trees. Plus always make the cut 1” above a node. Once it starts growing then fertilize with a liquid fertilizer at half strength once a month March- Oct. These trees do greatly benefit from pruning. If the rootball is more than 2” bigger than the pot and it looks like it might be but some trees hardly have any roots only then do you put in a pot that’s 2” bigger. These trees prefer clay pots or nursery pots. Ceramic aren’t advised
