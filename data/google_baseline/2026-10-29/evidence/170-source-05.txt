        - button "Kim Pehi":

        - generic: As mentioned, these are succulents. Succulents are storing water in their leaves and structures. The last thing they want is to be left in a substrate that stays wet. They will rot. I make my own substrate 50/50 Cactus soil and pumice. I almost always use terracotta pots. All of your pots should drain well and dry out fast. The pale color you see in the middle of your aloe And the space between the leaves on the other plants is etiolation from lack of enough light. This is what your lace aloe would look like with more sun /light. It's a misconception that succulents don't like water. Every plant needs water to survive. What they want is to dry out quickly. I soak and dry my plants. Pour enough water so that it goes right through your pot and starts to drain out the bottom. Soak and dry. Acclimate into a LOT more light. Glass is not a good option for succulents
