        - generic: "That’s a really good question, and it’s completely normal to feel nervous with your first Monstera Albo since they behave differently from regular Monsteras. Here’s a detailed comment you can use:"
        - generic: When an albo puts out a fully white leaf, it may look stunning, but unfortunately, it can’t photosynthesize. That means it’s not contributing any energy to the plant and will eventually fade or crisp up. In fact, if the plant keeps producing too many white leaves, it can weaken over time because it’s relying only on the green portions to make food.
        - generic: "You have two options here:"
        - generic: 1. Leave it for now – Some people prefer to leave a new white leaf until it naturally starts to decline. This won’t hurt anything short term, and the plant will usually push out another leaf soon. You’ll still want to watch closely what the next leaf looks like, because the pattern of variegation often depends on what’s stored in the node.
        - generic: 2. Trim it back – If you want to help the plant conserve its energy, you can trim the white leaf off now. This redirects energy to the stem and roots, encouraging healthier growth with more green balance.
        - generic: The real key is to pay attention to the node the leaf came from. If that node is heavily variegated, it may continue producing whiter leaves. If you start getting a streak of all-white growth, that’s when people usually cut back to a node with more green to prevent the plant from exhausting itself.
        - generic: Since this is a young cutting, I’d suggest letting it focus on rooting and stabilizing first, while you monitor the next growth. If the next leaf is also very white, then it’s worth cutting back to encourage a greener balance. With albos, it’s all about managing the variegation so you get that beautiful mix of white and green while keeping the plant strong.
        - generic: So don’t worry—you didn’t do anything wrong. This happens with many albos, and with a little patience and balance, your plant should grow into something beautiful.
