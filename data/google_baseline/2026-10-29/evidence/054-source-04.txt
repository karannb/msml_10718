    - paragraph: This plant features beautiful color combinations, and its variegation gives the illusion of a flower garden indoors. While it isn’t actually flowering, it offers that same colorful charm. It also produces a small spray of flowers during its growth cycle.

    - paragraph: The plant is native to Madagascar and grows more quickly in its natural climate, but it still performs very well indoors and offers vibrant color to your indoor garden.
    - paragraph:
      - text: "## Lighting"
      - text: Provide bright, indirect light for best color and to keep the plant from becoming leggy. In low light, the plant will stretch toward the light and become tall, lanky, and unattractive.
    - paragraph: Good locations include near an east-facing window or a southern window with filtered light. Avoid west-facing windows, which may scorch the delicate foliage. Full-spectrum lighting is also excellent for these plants, helping maintain vibrant color and healthy foliage.
    - paragraph:
      - text: "## Watering"
      - text: Polka Dot Plants are tropical and wilt quickly if under-watered. The good news is that they often bounce back if watered promptly, although they may lose a few leaves.
    - paragraph: Always water with warm or tepid water—never cold. These plants especially dislike cold water.
    - paragraph:
      - text: "## Fertilizing"
      - text: Fertilize monthly, except in December and January when growth naturally slows. Organic fertilizers are recommended to avoid burning the plant’s delicate roots. My Green Gourmet Houseplant Food works well, as do organic liquids following package directions.
    - paragraph:
      - text: "## Humidity & Temperature"
      - text: These plants love humidity. Ideal humidity is 45–65%, and temperatures should be between 65–85°F.
    - paragraph: A hygrometer is an excellent tool to monitor your environment. Plants also humidify each other, making groupings beneficial. Humidity trays and occasional misting help as well.
    - paragraph:
      - text: "## Soil"
      - text: Use a rich, well-draining soil with peat or coir for moisture retention, and perlite or pumice for drainage. If your soil holds too much water, add pumice or perlite to balance it.
    - paragraph:
      - text: "## Pruning"
      - text: Regular pruning is essential, even with bright light. Growth can become lanky, so pinch or prune back frequently—weekly in warm months, every 2–4 weeks during slower growth.
    - paragraph: Pinch just above a node to encourage fuller, bushier growth.
    - paragraph: These simple care routines will keep your Polka Dot Plant vibrant, healthy, and a standout in your indoor garden.

    - heading "Polka Dot Plant (Hypoestes) Care FAQ" [level=3]
    - heading "What type of light does a Polka Dot Plant need?" [level=4]
    - paragraph: The Polka Dot Plant thrives in bright, indirect light. This keeps the foliage vibrant and prevents the plant from becoming leggy or lanky.
    - heading "How often should I water my Polka Dot Plant?" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Water regularly to keep the soil evenly moist. The plant wilts quickly when dry, but it often recovers if watered promptly. Always use warm or tepid water.
    - heading "Does the Polka Dot Plant need high humidity?" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Yes. It prefers 45–65% humidity and grows best in warm, moderately humid environments. Grouping plants, using humidity trays, or misting helps.
    - heading "What temperature range is best for Polka Dot Plants?" [level=4]
    - paragraph: These plants do well between 65–85°F. Avoid cold drafts or overly dry, hot areas.
    - heading "How often should I fertilize a Polka Dot Plant?" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Fertilize once a month from February through November using an organic fertilizer to avoid root burn. Allow the plant to rest in December and January.
    - heading "What soil is best for Polka Dot Plants?" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Use a rich, well-draining soil with peat or coir for moisture retention and perlite or pumice for improved drainage.
    - heading "How do I keep my Polka Dot Plant bushy?" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Pinch or prune regularly, especially during the growing season. Prune just above a node to encourage fuller, bushier growth.
