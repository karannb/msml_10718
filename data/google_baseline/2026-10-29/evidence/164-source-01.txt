        - generic: These root in water. You can cut off the trunk, put in a vase of water and it will root. I started with 2 and have had many babies and given away more than I can count. Just an FYI, the leaves will make cats sick. Won't kill then unless they eat a bunch, but they will make a cat sick. This particular one is a red leaf edge dracaena also called a Madagascar dragon tree. They use regular soil/potting mix. They like bright light, but not direct light. I water my large one every 10 days or so, my littles more often. Twice a year I put in the bath tub and flood them multiple times and rinse off the leaves. Hardy guys. Since this one was stressed out so much, I would highly recommend cutting and rooting in water. I'm currently rooting two that had similar situation to yours. To much sun, heat and no soil.
