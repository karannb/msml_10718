      - generic: "How I care for my Anthurium clarinervium 🌿 Here are a few things that have been working for me, hopefully they can help! I’d rather let it dry a little than keep the roots constantly wet. 🪴 Potting mix: I use my regular chunky aroid mix — coco coir, orchid bark, perlite, worm castings, and charcoal. Those thicker roots do really well in something airy and well-draining, with plenty of space around the roots. 💦 Humidity: This is one of the Anthuriums that I’ve found does really well in ambient humidity. For me, consistent watering and good airflow have been much more important. 🍃 Imperfect leaves: Don’t panic over every little imperfection. Anthurium leaves don’t have to look perfect all the time, and a little damage doesn’t always mean something is wrong with the plant. If I could only recommend one Anthurium to grow, this would probably be it."
