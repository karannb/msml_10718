  - text: THE BASICS -Basically, all we are doing is finding a nice looking piece of the plant, chopping it off, pulling off the bottom leaves to leave a stem and sticking it in some dry soil. QUICK TIPS -Best time to do this is in the spring -water your succulents a few days before doing this so that they are fully hydrated. AFTER CARE TIPS -Wait at least 4-7 days before watering newly propagated succulents. -keep them in bright indirect light rather than direct sun until they are rooted. LEAF PROPAGATION -place leaves of a tray of moist soil -keep soil moist (spray bottles work well) -be patient. Eventually, roots will form and new baby plants will grow! THANKS FOR WATCHING!
