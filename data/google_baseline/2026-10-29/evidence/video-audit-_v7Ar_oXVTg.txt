  - generic: The white mold that you have found in your house plants soil is just mold that’s feeding on decaying matter in your soil. This is pretty normal and consider not a big deal for house plants. It does look unsightly but that’s about the only harm it’s going to do. Your house plant soil is full of decaying matter and that’s what dirt essentially is so they have a little bit of mold on the top of it isn’t abnormal. To get rid of the white mold on your house plants soil you can just take your finger and start mashing up the mold. You can literally just blend this into the soil that’s already in the pot. If this really bothers you you can also take a metal spoon and sort of just remove it from the pot itself. It’s very easy to physically removed from the soil and you can just move on with your day after removing it.
