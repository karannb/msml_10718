        - generic: This Dracaena has lived its entire life with insufficient lighting. If it were mine to rehab, I would transfer to a slightly smaller pot entirely full of soil, cut the stem to about 6”, and give it some brighter light (not direct sun, but very bright) and water when top inch of soil is dry. Some extra humidity wouldn’t hurt. You can trim the growing tip to about 3 leaves and propagate in water or leca, then add back to the pot. The stem in the pot should grow a new side stem/leaves, though not necessarily fast. This google image shows a similar plant, so you have a goal! Good luck with the rescue!
