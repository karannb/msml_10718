        - generic: That looks like an alocasia zebrina. Yellowing could be due to too much water or not enough water. Let it dry out 2/3 of the way before watering again. If it’s not due to over watering it could be under watering as well. If they go without water too long they will do this as well. Usually gets a little crispy around the edges. They also don’t like tap water honestly. If you can give them filtered water or rain water you collect it will be happier.
