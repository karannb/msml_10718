      - generic: The top picture shows False Oxlip, not true Oxlip. False Oxlip is a natural hybrid between Primrose and Cowslip and is also the origin of the Polyanthus hybrids that are widely planted in parks and cemeteries. In Britain, Oxlip occurs only in a small area of East Anglia and is a woodland species. The flowers are wider than the ones in your photo and tend to form a more or less one-sided head. http://webidguides.com/_templates/group_primula.html.
