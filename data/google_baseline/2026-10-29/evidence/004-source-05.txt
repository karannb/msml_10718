        - generic: Is it in another pot within the clay pot such that excess water can drain away into gravel or styrofoam peanuts, etc.? If not, you should repot to create such a situation; that way you can check half hour after watering to determine if the inner pot is sitting in water and if it is then dump that water out. Dieffenbachias like bright to medium indirect light. Check regularly for spider mites and other insects; mites are usually on under side of leaves and smaller than a pinhead, and create fine webbing usually near base of leaves & between leaves. They cause very fine blotchiness of leaves because they suck juice out of leaves. Spray with miticide or insecticidal soap if you have any.
