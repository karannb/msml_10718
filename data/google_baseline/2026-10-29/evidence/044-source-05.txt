        - generic: Since the plant is new to you and it's like that already, there are chances of root rot.
        - generic: So I'd personally unpot to check the bulb and roots for rot to give me a peace of mind.
        - generic: Leaves don't matter when it comes to alocadias only the bulb is important.
        - generic: As soon as that is healthy (firm and not mushy) the plant is very much alive and when acclimated and given the right care it will start producing lots of leaves.
        - generic: I'd check for rot, remove any soft or very dried out unhealthy roots and check the bulb.
        - generic: If healthy root system left is enough I'd pot ut up again.
        - generic: If not, I'd reroot in moist perlite.
        - generic: I water thoroughly in the tub when 75% of the potting medium is dry.
        - generic: Bright indirect light and a few hours of direct morning sun.
        - generic: Check for spider mites often and fertilize when the plant has active growth.
