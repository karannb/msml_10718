  - paragraph: From the description, I assume the stem broke from the base and detached from the roots.
  - paragraph: Normally rooting in a "wet" box (covered to retain moisture) is ideal, especially that the leaves are already wilted. You may resort to rooting it in water (while also covered to retain moisture) for best results.
  - paragraph: If a stump was left above the ground, where the stem broke-off, there is a chance it will leaf-out again and regrow. Don't give up on this one yet.
