        - generic: Palms natural environment is marshy bog area. They definitely need constant moisture but enough of a chunky soil to aerate. The pot as said above is way too big and can cause your plant to rot. Your pot should only be 1-2 inches bigger than your root ball. I would downsize the pot and mix some orchid bark and perlite in to the soil to make it chunky and less dense. As far as watering goes I literally run a gallon of water through mine every two days.
