If your philodendron leaves are turning brown and it's not because of too much water, it could be because of the light it's getting. Philodendrons like indirect light, and they usually do well with artificial light too. If you've placed your philodendron in direct sunlight, like near a window or outside, its leaves might turn yellow or get sunburned. On the other hand, if your philodendron doesn't get enough light, especially in darker rooms or during winter, its leaves might start to turn yellow as well. In that case, moving it closer to a window could help it stay healthy.
