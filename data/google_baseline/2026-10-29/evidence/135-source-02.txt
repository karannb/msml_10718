      - generic: How to fix yellowing leaves by repotting root-bound plants
      - generic: Titles are generated by AI from Meta
      - heading "Zone 5B" [level=3]:
        - strong: Zone 5B
      - generic: Figured I'd hop on since I had to do a repot on a couple things that were telling me they wanted a bigger pot...so figured I'd share some advice for new growers thinking what's wrong with my plant!?..
      - generic: If you've had a plant for a while and it was growing good, then all of a sudden old leaves start yellowing/dying but New growth is healthy 99% of the time it's just getting crowded and it needs to go up in pot size or watered more frequently + replenishing the nutrients (if you wanted to continue growing root bound)..
      - generic: Simply pull the plant out of the pot, if it's snug go up in pot size..add soil to the bottom, sit plant in, add remaining soil around and pat the pot to settle, done.. remember less is more! no need to disturb the roots (cause stress, The Roots will always find their way to new soil no matter how root bound!)
      - generic: Only time you want to disturb the roots is if you're doing a prune(best to prune a couple days after a watering) pull out of pot, chop the bottom 10-15% off, add fresh soil to original pot, place back in(perfect fit) don't water for a couple days or at least wait the following day for the roots to heal shut properly (you don't want them to rot or get infected, that's why it's best to prune a couple days after watering instead of when it's dry and thirsty).
      - generic: Either way you choose, remember less is always more!! Plants are extraordinary, they communicate with us, if you can learn to read a plant you will never have any issues! So pay attention to the signs they give and learn as you grow!
