        - button "Steve Hawkins":

        - generic: Hi! Steve at Specialty Tropicals here; looks like a Snake Plant (Dracaena, formerly Sansavieria) called ‘Emerald Star’ from what I can tell.
        - generic: It would normally be deep-green, but the form of the leaves and the prominent midrib make me think that’s what it is.
        - generic: Looks like it needs bright light (part-day full sun is good for these, but I’d ease this one into it, since it doesn’t look healthy right now.
        - generic: What’s the soil mix look like? They grow best in a mix that’s chunky, holding moisture, but also letting the roots breathe. Ours are grown in a mix of 75% peat-substitute (a treated wood fiber that takes the place of peat moss) and the other 25% consists of coarse sand, fir bark, and chunky perlite. They actually like a fair bit of moisture, but they need a potting medium that drains and allows air circulation, to prevent root rot.
        - generic: In our production greenhouses, they get watered daily and get near-full sun, and they’re very happy with that.
        - generic: Best of luck with it—they’re beautiful plants!
