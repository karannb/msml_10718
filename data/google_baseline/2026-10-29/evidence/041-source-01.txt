      - generic: I keep having an ongoing issue with multiple new Alocasias. When I buy them, they look great, but they seem to be potted in very dense soil or a mixture that just does not seem to dry out very quickly. I don’t want to repot them right away and cause them more stress when I’ve just bought them, but they start to droop, yellow and wilt and two of them have died before I even water them once with the soil still wet. The third one that started doing this I ended up repotting it just to see if I could save it and it died anyway. Any ideas? Obviously they were doing fine at the nursery in the soil, but it’s like as soon as I bring them home they won’t dry out. ￼
