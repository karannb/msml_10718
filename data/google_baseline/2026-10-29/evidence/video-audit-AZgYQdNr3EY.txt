  - text: The leaves on your ZZ plant are turning yellow because of overwatering. This house plant absolutely hates having too much water in its soil. This plant is used to drought seasons in natural habitat, and that consists of completely drying out in between watering‘s. Your leaves on your ZZ plant are turning yellow because the roots are sitting in too much moisture. To fix this issue, you should remove the root structure from the pot and take a look at it. Try to do a visual scan and see if the roots are starting to rot away. You will also be able to tell because if the roots are starting to rot, there should be a smell coming from the soil.
