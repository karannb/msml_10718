  - text: Over-misting can cause water burn, where too much moisture damages the plant's leaves or roots.Water burning appears as brown spots surrounded by yellow rings on the leaves. It could also be a leaf spot disease. When a plant gets Leaf Spot disease, it means a fungus or bacteria is attacking it, causing brown spots edged in yellow where it feeds on the leaves. These spots can vary in shape, color, and size. To manage Leaf Spot disease and similar fungal or bacterial issues, ensure good air flow around the plant, use well-draining soil, keep leaves dry, and avoid overwatering. If you suspect Leaf Spot, avoid misting the plant.
