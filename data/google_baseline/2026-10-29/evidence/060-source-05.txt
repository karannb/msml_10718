  - paragraph:
    - text: Are you noticing yellow leaves on your
    - link "Syngonium":
      - /url: https://plantproper.com/collections/syngonium
    - text: and wondering, “Why is my Syngonium turning yellow?” At Plant Proper, we understand how frustrating it can be when your cherished houseplant starts to lose its vibrant color. Below, we’ll discuss the main reasons behind yellowing leaves and how to address them, ensuring your Syngonium stays healthy and lush.
  - heading "1. Overwatering and Root Rot" [level=2]
  - paragraph: "One of the most common causes of yellow leaves in indoor plants is overwatering. Giving your Syngonium too much water leads to root rot, preventing the roots from absorbing oxygen. This results in mushy roots and yellow, wilted leaves. To avoid this:"
  - list:
    - listitem [level=1]: "Check Soil Moisture: Water only when the top 50% of the soil is dry."
    - listitem [level=1]: "Improve Drainage: Ensure your pot has drainage holes and use a well-draining potting mix."
  - paragraph: If you’re wondering, “Why is my Syngonium turning yellow?”, start by assessing your watering schedule. Balancing hydration is essential—enough water to keep the soil moist, but not so much that your plant drowns.
  - heading "2. Light Issues" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Another factor behind yellow leaves is insufficient or excessive light. Every Syngonium variety has its own light preferences, but generally, they enjoy bright, indirect light. Too little light can hinder photosynthesis, leading to pale or yellow leaves. Too much direct sunlight can scorch the foliage, creating yellow or brown patches. Find a spot with filtered light to keep your plant thriving.
  - heading "3. Pot Size and Root Bound Plants" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Sometimes, a Syngonium that has outgrown its pot will display yellow leaves. When roots have limited space, they struggle to absorb nutrients. If your plant appears cramped, consider repotting it into a slightly larger container. This gives the roots room to expand and helps the plant maintain healthy foliage.
